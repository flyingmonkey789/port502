How it works

Here’s how stories make it into the feed.

  1. 01
    Aggregate Agent

    Checks government advisories, OT research teams, trade press, and targeted news searches every hour for new stories.

  2. 02
    Correlation Agent

    Deduplicates coverage and ranks each story against a defined set of relevance criteria to filter out vendor fluff, low-severity alerts, and noise.

  3. 03
    Publishing Agent

    Publishes the highest-ranked stories to this feed, each linked to its original source.
